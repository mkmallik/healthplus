Features
Everything you need to
track your health
From food to fitness, habits to reminders — HealthPlus covers every aspect of your daily health journey.
🍴

AI Food Logging

Snap a photo, speak, or type your meals. GPT-4o Vision identifies foods and estimates calories, macros, and portions — with Indian cuisine expertise.

🎤

Universal Voice

One voice command does it all. Say "I had 2 rotis and dal" or "Ran 5k this morning" — AI classifies and routes to the right tracker automatically.

🏃

Exercise Tracking

Log workouts via voice or text. MET-based calorie estimation, intensity detection, muscle group identification, and smart exercise summaries.

👣

Step Tracking

Enter steps manually or snap your watch screen — GPT-4o Vision reads the count. Steps over 8,000 auto-create a walking exercise entry.

⚖

Body Metrics

Track weight, waist, and biceps over time. Natural language input — just say "75.2 kg today" and the AI extracts the measurement.

🔥

Habits & Streaks

Three habit types: boolean check-ins, descriptive logs with photos, and todo lists. Automatic streaks, daily progress, and carry-over tasks.

📈

Insights & Trends

Weekly and monthly dashboards with bar charts, calorie trends, step trends, weight progress, burned calorie breakdown (BMR + exercise + steps).

🔔

Smart Reminders

Set reminders by voice — "remind me at 5pm to drink water". Auto-generates spoken TTS audio that plays at the scheduled time.

📝

Daily Notes

Journal your day with text, voice recordings, and photos. Search across notes, browse by date range with multi-day views.

  • ✓ Todo integration — Voice reminders can also create a todo item alongside the alarm
  • ✓ Playback control — Tap the play button to preview any reminder's audio anytime
  • ✓ Date-aware — Reminders are tied to specific dates, visible in your todo screen
